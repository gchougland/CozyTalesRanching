# Sources: models-b (Cow Barn, Sheepfold, Trough)

All three models and atlases are generated by the scripts in this folder; nothing is copied from
vanilla and no generator was used. fal spend: US$0.00.

| File | Made by |
| --- | --- |
| `Common/Blocks/CozyRanch/Houses/CowBarn/CowBarn.blockymodel`, `CowBarn_Texture.png` | `node art/build/models-b/cowbarn.mjs` |
| `Common/Blocks/CozyRanch/Houses/Sheepfold/Sheepfold.blockymodel`, `Sheepfold_Texture.png` | `node art/build/models-b/sheepfold.mjs` |
| `Common/Blocks/CozyRanch/Trough/Trough.blockymodel`, `Trough_Filled.blockymodel`, `Trough_Texture.png` | `node art/build/models-b/trough.mjs` |
| `Common/Icons/ItemsGenerated/CozyRanch_{House_CowBarn,House_Sheepfold,Trough}.png` | `model_render` three-quarter at 256 on `#00000000`, then `icon-downscale.mjs` (premultiplied box average to 64) |

Textures are painted procedurally (`materials.mjs`): smooth value noise in clusters of 2+ texels,
one-texel grooves 10 to 16 L* darker and cooler, light from above added per face in `lib.mjs`.
Palettes come from `art/concepts/DESIGN_NOTES.md`.
