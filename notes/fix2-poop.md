# fix2-poop: poop piles

Branch `wave/fix2-poop`. Gibson asked for animals that now and then leave poop on the ground. The poop is placeable
and breaks into poop. Piles near each other merge, so they never fill up the land.

## What changed

- **The block** is `CozyRanch_Poop_Pile` (`Server/Item/Items/CozyRanch/Poop/`). It is a soft-serve mound of stacked
  octagon layers, each layer turned a little to make a swirl. It is painted warm brown, with a lit lip and a cool crease on
  every layer and a wet sheen on the tops.
  - It has three models in `Common/Blocks/CozyRanch/Poop/`, all with whole sizes:

    | Size | Droppings | Blocks (w x h) | Nodes | Atlas |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Small | 1 | 0.31 x 0.35 | 15 | 64 x 64 |
    | Medium | 2 to 3 | 0.54 x 0.45 (a second little swirl) | 29 | 64 x 128 |
    | Large | 4 to 8 | 0.75 x 0.58 (a broad base, three swirls, three straw bits) | 46 | 128 x 128 |

  - The block is like vanilla `Ingredient_Poop`'s placed block. It uses `Material: Empty`, so nothing collides with it
    and animals and players walk through. Its hitbox is the vanilla `Plant_Small` (`Plant_Medium` from 4 droppings), and
    that hitbox is only for aiming.
  - Breaking it is `Soft`, which means one hit by hand. Its support rule needs a whole face below. It has a random yaw
    and uses the Mud sounds and particles.
  - From 4 droppings it also shows the vanilla `Flies_Poop` particles, coming from the pile itself.
  - It is generated by `tools/poop/build.mjs`, which uses the art3 library in the main checkout's `art/build/art3`. The
    icon comes from `tools/poop/icon.mjs`: a 64 x 64 render of the medium pile.
- **Where the count lives.** The count lives in the block state. The default state is 1, and the states `Count2` to
  `Count8` hold 2 to 8. Each state names its model and has its own `Soft` drop list,
  `Server/Drops/CozyRanch/Poop/Drops_CozyRanch_Poop_<n>.json`, which drops exactly n vanilla `Ingredient_Poop`.
  - So the count is saved with the chunk and survives restarts with no save file and nothing to fall out of step.
  - The drops come from data, with no Java on break.
  - Players cannot get a state other than the default.
  - This is the same pattern as the vanilla crops.
- **The item.** It is the same id as the block. Its recipe is 1 `Ingredient_Poop` → 1 pile at the Farmingbench
  (Farming), and breaking a pile gives the poop back, one for one.
  - I chose a recipe over a silk-touch rule because Hytale has no silk touch. A recipe also keeps one obvious loop and
    never lets a player duplicate poop.
- **Animals** (`produce/PoopService`, `PoopSystem`, `PoopRules`, `PoopTuning`).
  - A timer adds up time per world. Every `PassSeconds` (15) it queues one pass with `world.execute`, so no block write
    happens in a tick.
  - A pass walks the record indexes for each house's residents. Every condition below must hold:
    - The animal is owned, housed and ACTIVE (outside its house).
    - Its entity is loaded.
    - It is not a baby (`Babies`) and its species is on (`Species`; the frog is off).
    - It is daytime (0.25 to 0.75).
    - It has cooled down (`CooldownMinutes` 6 real minutes).
    - It wins a roll at `ChancePerMinute` (0.12), converted to a chance per pass.
  - A pass makes at most `MaxPilesPerPass` (4) piles.
- **Merge rule.** The pass reads the cells within `MergeRadius` (3, a square, one cell up or down) of the animal's feet,
  and only from loaded sections.
  - The nearest pile that is not full gains one, including a pile the animal is standing in.
  - If every pile near is full (`MaxCount` 8), nothing happens.
  - With no pile near, a new pile goes in the feet cell, but only if all of these hold:
    - The cell is empty and dry, with no fluid in it or below it.
    - The cell below is solid and not a filler of a multi-cell block.
    - The id below contains none of `ExcludedGround`: Farmland, Tilled, Path, Plant_Crop, CozyRanch_, Wood_ (floors)
      and Furniture_.
    - The cell is more than `DoorClearance` (2) from any house door.
    - The house has fewer than `MaxPilesPerHouse` (6) piles.
- **Index.** Each world keeps an index of the pile cells it has seen, in memory only.
  - After a start, the index is rebuilt for one house at a time, the first time that house's piles are counted. This is
    one scan of home range + merge radius around the door, 9 cells high. It is about 6,900 reads and about 4 ms, once
    per house per start.
  - After that, each count checks every entry with one read and drops the entries that are gone (broken piles).
  - A normal pass is about 300 reads and 0.3 to 0.5 ms with two cows.
- **Players.** `PoopSystem.Place` handles a player placing the pile item.
  - It refuses the pile in water, on excluded ground or on a building, and says so in chat
    (`cozyranch_produce.poop.place.refused`). The engine's support rule covers the rest.
  - With `PlayerPilesMerge` (true), a placed pile next to a pile that is not full joins it: in a world task afterwards,
    the new block goes and the old pile shows one more, so the item is not lost. Otherwise it stays where it was put.
- **Tuning** is in `Server/CozyTalesRanching/Poop.json`. A `Poop.json` in the plugin data folder overrides it, and it is
  reloaded by `/ranch reload` (through `ProduceFeature.reload`). The global switch is `Enabled`.
- **Admin commands** live under the existing `/ranch produce`, so `RanchCommand` is unchanged. Every leaf needs
  `cozyranch.admin`.
  - `/ranch produce poop status`: the known piles with their counts and sizes, and the cost of the last pass.
  - `/ranch produce poop now`: every eligible animal makes a dropping now. It ignores the chance, the cooldown, the time
    of day and the switch, but never the ground, door, merge and house rules.
  - `/ranch produce poop here`: one dropping at your feet with an animal's rules.
  - `/ranch produce poop break`: breaks the nearest pile through the engine's own bare-hand block damage, then counts
    the poop items it dropped.
  - `/ranch produce poop logon|logoff`: log every pile made or grown.
- **Lang:** item name and description, the refusal and the command texts are in `cozyranch_produce.lang`.

  - count ↔ block state id
  - size per count
  - merge into the nearest pile that is not full
  - full piles stop new ones
  - the radius and the one-cell-up-or-down limit
  - an animal standing in a pile grows it
  - a player's pile never merges with itself
  - the house limit stops new piles but not merging
  - the ground rules (water, farmland, path, floor, our blocks, building filler, taken cell)
  - who may poop
  - the per-pass chance adds up to the per-minute chance, and the cooldown (including a clock going back)

## Not seen or not exercised

- Nobody has seen the piles in the client: the models, the flies and the random yaw, walking through a pile, or a
  break by a real click. The break went through the engine's own block-damage call, not a client packet.
- A player placing the item (the place check and player merge) was not run. The bot cannot place blocks, so only the
  rules behind it are unit tested.
- Restart persistence was not run live. Each test server starts a fresh universe. The count is the block state, which
  the engine saves with the chunk.
- Night gating, excluded ground and water were not run live (they are unit tested). In these test worlds the cows stood
  still by the barn (see `notes/behaviour.md`, adult cow pathing), so every pile landed in one spot.
- In two of the three runs the bot ended on the respawn page. The bot died somewhere in setup; I did not chase it,
  because nothing here hurts players.
